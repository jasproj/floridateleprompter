# How Much Does a Teleprompter Cost in Florida? 2026 Rates

Most clients ask the same first question: what will a teleprompter cost for my event? Below are the ranges we actually quote in Florida, what moves the price up or down, and what is included. Every job gets a written quote before you commit, with crew and travel shown as separate lines.

*Rates as of October 2026.*

## Typical Rates

| Service | Typical range |
|---------|---------------|
| Operator, half day (up to 4 hours) | $600 – $800 |
| Operator, full day (up to 10 hours) | $1,200 – $1,500 |
| Operator plus full equipment package, including presidential glass | $1,500 – $2,000 per day |
| Overtime past 10 hours | billed hourly, shown on the quote |


## What Changes the Price

- **Multi-day events (3+ days):** 10% off the day rate.
- **Weekends and holidays:** +25%.
- **Rush bookings (under 48 hours' notice):** +50%.
- **Travel:** we are based in Tampa Bay. Jobs within 50 miles carry no mileage; beyond that we bill mileage, plus a per diem on overnight trips. Travel is listed separately and never folded into the day rate.
- **Equipment:** a camera-mount prompter for a video shoot costs less than presidential glass, confidence monitors and a stage setup for a keynote.

## What Is Included

- An experienced operator who loads, formats and runs your script live
- Setup and testing before doors or call time
- A rehearsal pass with each speaker
- Live edits when the script changes at the last minute
- Coordination with your house AV or production company

## How Booking Works

1. Send the date, venue, number of speakers and the setup you need.
2. We send a written quote that lists operator, equipment, travel and any overtime terms.
3. A 30–50% deposit holds the date. The balance is invoiced after the event.

## Ways to Keep Costs Down

- **Book early.** Two weeks' notice avoids rush pricing; allow three to four weeks during political season.
- **Bundle days.** Three or more consecutive days earn the multi-day discount.
- **Send a near-final script early.** Less reformatting on site means less time on the clock.
- **Tell us what your AV company already provides.** If they supply monitors or switching, we do not duplicate it.

We cover Tampa Bay, Orlando, Miami, Jacksonville, Sarasota and the rest of Florida. Ranges only go so far. Send us your event details and we will come back with an itemized quote, usually the same day.
