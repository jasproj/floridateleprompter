Events and Venues in St. Petersburg

Downtown St. Petersburg packs a lot of event space into a few walkable blocks. The Mahaffey Theater at the Duke Energy Center for the Arts is the city's main stage for galas and large keynotes, and The Vinoy Resort & Golf Club beside the marina hosts the ballroom dinners and executive retreats that go with them. The St. Pete Pier draws outdoor announcements and branded video, while the Dalí Museum and the Museum of Fine Arts lend their galleries to donor receptions.

Tropicana Field anchors the south end of downtown for sports productions and large corporate gatherings. USF St. Petersburg, Johns Hopkins All Children's Hospital, and Bayfront Health St. Petersburg generate commencements, foundation dinners, and recorded leadership messages, and Jabil's headquarters brings Fortune 500 corporate communications work to the city. What those jobs typically need:

  • Theater galas: presidential glass at the lectern, operator at front-of-house on a wired feed
  • Hotel ballrooms: floor-standing confidence monitors that tuck behind the centerpieces
  • Waterfront shoots: high-brightness camera-mount units that stay readable in direct sun
