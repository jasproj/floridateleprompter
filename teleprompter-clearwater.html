Events and Venues in Clearwater

Ruth Eckerd Hall is the big room in Clearwater, and its stage sees as many corporate meetings and nonprofit award nights as it does touring acts. Downtown, the rebuilt Coachman Park and The Sound amphitheater give the city a waterfront setting for festivals, concerts, and civic ceremonies, and the Nancy and David Bilheimer Capitol Theatre on Cleveland Street handles mid-sized programs. The Clearwater Marine Aquarium hosts donor events and film shoots on Island Estates.

Across the causeway, Opal Sands Resort, the Wyndham Grand Clearwater Beach, and the Sheraton Sand Key are where conference planners put multi-day meetings. On the business side, BayCare Health System and TD SYNNEX are both headquartered in Clearwater, Morton Plant Hospital runs its own foundation events, and BayCare Ballpark hosts spring training and community programs. Typical setups for these rooms:

  • Beachfront ballrooms: confidence monitors on short stands, kept clear of the windows and glare
  • Outdoor amphitheater: daylight-readable monitors and a shaded operator position
