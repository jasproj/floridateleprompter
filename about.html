About Florida Teleprompter

Professional teleprompting services across Florida, backed by 25+ years of national experience.

Our Story

Florida Teleprompter brings professional teleprompting services to the Sunshine State as part of the National Teleprompter network. With over 25 years of experience serving clients across the country, we deliver the same level of excellence that has made us trusted partners for Fortune 500 companies, sports organizations, broadcast networks, and production companies nationwide.

Our work across Florida represents our commitment to providing local expertise backed by national resources. Whether you're producing a corporate event in Tampa, a broadcast in Orlando, or a conference in Miami, you get experienced operators, current-generation equipment, and seamless execution.

What Sets Us Apart

We're not just equipment providers — we're teleprompting specialists. Our operators are seasoned professionals who understand timing, pace, and presence. We work directly with talent to ensure smooth, confident performances whether you're at a live event with thousands of attendees or in a studio shooting a promotional video.

Every show benefits from our network's combined experience: thousands of successful productions, consistent standards across markets, and the flexibility to scale from single-operator shoots to multi-city coordinated events.

Our Services

We provide full-service teleprompting for:

  • Live Events — Trade shows, conferences, political rallies, sports events, and concerts. Real-time script adjustment and flawless delivery under pressure.
  • Corporate Productions — Executive communications, shareholder meetings, training videos, and internal broadcasts.
  • Video Production — Infomercials, promotional videos, social content, and scripted shoots.
  • Broadcast — News studios, talk shows, and live broadcasts with seamless system integration.
