Event Types

What We Run During Campaign Season

Every booking includes the operator, the presidential glass, a backup system, script prep and live edits. One line item, no hourly meter.

Rallies & Stump Speeches

Indoor or outdoor. Glass panels sized for a podium, a high-bright on-camera unit for daylight stages, and an operator desk that runs off our own feed so it can sit wherever the advance team wants it.

Best for: candidate rallies, GOTV events, endorsements

Election Night

Victory and concession speeches loaded side by side, a holding script for the wait, and an operator on headset with the campaign manager. We do not leave at a scheduled end time; we leave when the speech is done.

Best for: watch parties, results-night addresses

Fundraisers, Galas & Town Halls

Multiple speakers, one run list. Host, candidate, surrogates and the ask, each on its own cue. Script changes from the table go in live without anyone on stage noticing.

Best for: dinners, PAC events, party conventions, debates

How It Works

Built for Campaign Schedules

1. Send the date and venue

Call or use the form. You get a per-event number the same day. A deposit holds the date; the balance is due after the event.

2. Scripts whenever they are ready

Day before is ideal, an hour before is normal, mid-speech edits happen. Any format: Word, PDF, Google Doc, a text message from the speechwriter.

3. We load in early and run it

Set, tested and rehearsed before doors. The operator paces to the speaker, not the other way around, and stays through strike.

Venue needs: power at the podium and at the operator desk, ideally within about 50 feet of the stage. We bring everything else, including SDI/HDMI feeds to the house AV if the venue wants to mirror the prompter.

FAQ

Political Teleprompter Questions

Can you handle a script that changes an hour before the speech?

Yes. Campaign scripts change up to the minute. Send drafts in any format; we load, format and edit live from the desk, including cuts and inserts while the speaker is already on stage.

Do you work election night?

Yes. Victory and concession speeches loaded and ready, a holding script for the wait, and we stay until the speaker is done.

Is the prompter visible to the audience or cameras?

No. The speaker reads the script in two clear glass panels beside the podium; the room and the cameras see a speaker looking at them.

Can you cover multiple stops in one day?

Yes. Multi-stop days and statewide swings are quoted as a package built around load-in and strike times.

How much does it cost?

Single-speech events are quoted per event, operator and equipment included. Multi-day and multi-city schedules are quoted as a package. You get the number the same day.

How fast can you be on site?

Same-week and next-day bookings are routine in election season. Call 727-623-2652 and we will tell you right away if the date is open.
