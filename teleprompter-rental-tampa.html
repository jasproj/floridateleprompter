FAQ

Teleprompter Rental in Tampa — Questions

How much does teleprompter rental cost in Tampa?

Operator plus prompter runs $600–$800 for a half day and $1,200–$1,500 for a full day. A full day with the complete equipment package is $1,500–$2,000. No travel charge within 40 miles of Tampa.

Can I rent the teleprompter without an operator?

We rent with an operator. Live events and shoots go wrong at the prompter more than anywhere else, and the operator is what keeps the speaker on pace when the script changes at the last minute.

What equipment do you bring?

A 17" high-bright on-camera prompter, a presidential glass prompter for podium speeches, laptops and our own script software. We run on our own feed and don't need line of sight to the stage.

How far in advance should I book?

A week is comfortable. Same-week and next-day bookings happen often in Tampa; call and we'll tell you right away if the date is open. A 50% deposit holds it.

What do you need from the venue?

Power at the podium or camera position and power at the operator desk, ideally within about 50 feet of the stage. Scripts the day before are ideal, but we format on site routinely.

Do you cover St. Pete, Clearwater and Sarasota?

Yes. All of Pinellas and most of Pasco, Polk and Manatee fall inside the 40-mile no-travel-fee radius. Sarasota is a short mileage add. See Tampa Bay services and Sarasota.
