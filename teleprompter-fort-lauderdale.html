Events and Venues in Fort Lauderdale

The Broward County Convention Center is the center of gravity for large meetings in Fort Lauderdale, and the Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show each fall brings a week of press conferences and brand launches with it. The Broward Center for the Performing Arts downtown is the city's stage for galas and keynotes, while the Marriott Harbor Beach Resort and the other oceanfront hotels along A1A host multi-day corporate programs. The Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino in Hollywood is a short drive south for arena-scale events.

Port Everglades, one of the world's busiest cruise ports, makes the maritime industry a steady source of corporate video and live-event work. Nova Southeastern University in nearby Davie, Broward Health Medical Center, and headquarters such as AutoNation add commencements, foundation dinners, and executive video. What those jobs usually call for:

  • Convention general sessions: presidential glass or stage-edge monitors, operator at the production table
  • Theater galas: glass panels at the lectern with the operator on a wired feed from front-of-house
  • Cruise and corporate video: a through-the-lens rig that sets up in a ship conference room or office
